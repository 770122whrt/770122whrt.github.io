Hi, I'm **Haoyu Wang (王浩宇)**, a master's student in **Electronic Information at Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School (Tsinghua SIGS)**. I completed my undergraduate studies at **Zhejiang University**.

My research interests span two connected areas:

- **Reinforcement Learning & Robot Control.** I am interested in reinforcement learning (including PPO), Koopman-based dynamics models, model predictive control (MPC), and the challenges of transferring learned control methods from simulation to real robots (**sim-to-real**).
- **AI Agents & Agent Infrastructure.** I explore multi-agent collaboration, agent harness and tool orchestration, and the role of **memory** and **retrieval** in long-running, context-rich agent workflows.

### Selected projects

- **[Text2IFC / IFC Repair]({{ '/projects/text2ifc/' | relative_url }})** — language-driven structured 3D model generation and controlled editing, with explicit validation.
- **[EASYkoopman / AUV Control]({{ '/projects/easykoopman/' | relative_url }})** — Koopman-based dynamics prediction and MPC research in underwater robotics.
- **[WeAgent]({{ '/projects/weagent/' | relative_url }})** — a multi-agent collaboration project.
