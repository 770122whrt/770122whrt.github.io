Haoyu Wang

Master's student in Electronic Information · Tsinghua SIGS

Hi, I’m Haoyu Wang (王浩宇), a master’s student in Electronic Information at Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School (Tsinghua SIGS). I completed my undergraduate studies at Zhejiang University.

I’m interested in how intelligent systems perceive, decide, act, and adapt. My research interests span two directions:

Underwater Robotics: Perception, Control & Self-Evolution

How can underwater robots make reliable decisions with incomplete observations, uncertain dynamics, and changing environments? I am interested in combining onboard perception, the robot’s own motion state, and experience from interaction to support robust control and longer-term adaptation.

Possible methods include reinforcement learning (e.g., PPO), Koopman-based dynamics modeling, model predictive control (MPC), and sim-to-real adaptation.

AI Agents: Harness, Memory & Retrieval

How can agents maintain context across long tasks, find relevant information when needed, and learn from execution feedback? I am interested in agent harness design, memory and retrieval, tool orchestration, and multi-agent coordination, especially how these mechanisms support grounded decisions.

For research questions and ideas, see Research.

Selected Explorations

  • EASYkoopman / AUV Control — dynamics representations and model-based underwater robot control.
  • Text2IFC / IFC Repair — language-driven agents operating on structured 3D artifacts.
  • WeAgent — coordination among multiple agents.
